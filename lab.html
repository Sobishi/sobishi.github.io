OPERATIONS LAB

Building systems for better operational decisions.

A working space where I explore how supply chain operations, enterprise systems, analytics, automation and artificial intelligence can work together.

THE IDEA

Start with the operational problem.

Technology is not the starting point.

I start by understanding the operational problem: what is happening, why it is happening, what information is available and where the decision process breaks down.

From there, I explore whether process redesign, enterprise systems, analytics, automation or AI can produce a better outcome.

CURRENT BUILD

Operations Intelligence Agent

I am developing an AI-powered decision-support concept for supply chain and operations teams.

The objective is to move beyond simply displaying operational information and instead help users investigate problems, identify relevant information, understand possible root causes and determine what action should be taken.

01

Problem

Operational teams often spend significant time searching across ERP systems, spreadsheets, reports and emails before they can even begin analysing a problem.

02

Approach

Connect operational data, business rules and intelligent workflows so that recurring investigations can become structured and repeatable.

03

Direction

Build toward an agent that can investigate inventory, supplier, warehouse and procurement questions and support better operational decisions.

MY BUILDING METHOD

From problem to operational intelligence.

01

Understand

Define the operational problem, stakeholders, workflow, constraints and desired outcome.

02

Map

Understand the process, information flows, transactions, controls and decision points.

03

Analyse

Use data and analytical tools to identify patterns, exceptions, root causes and opportunities.

04

Build

Develop a practical solution using systems, automation, analytics or AI where appropriate.

05

Test

Validate the solution against realistic operational scenarios and measure whether it actually improves the outcome.

06

Document

Capture the reasoning, architecture, results and lessons learned so the work can be reused and improved.
