About Sulaimon

Supply chain and operations professional building toward the intersection of operations, enterprise systems, analytics and artificial intelligence.

MY JOURNEY

From running operations to building the systems behind them.

I am a supply chain and operations professional with eight years of experience across inventory, logistics, warehousing, planning, procurement and operational improvement.

My experience has largely been built in environments where operational decisions have real consequences: inventory availability, customer service, logistics cost, warehouse performance and supplier reliability.

Across Nigeria and Ghana, I have managed complex multi-site operations involving 11 warehouse and field stocking locations, a wider network of 111 stocking points, approximately 1,300 active SKUs and inventory valued at around $2 million.

One of the most significant transformations I led was improving inventory accuracy from 45% to 98%. The solution was not simply counting more stock. I focused on understanding how operational execution, processes and system transactions interacted.

That experience changed the way I think about supply chain problems.

I became increasingly interested in the layer between operations and technology: how systems are designed, how data is generated, how processes are translated into transactions and how better information can lead to better decisions.

WHY SYSTEMS

The problem I want to solve next.

Many operational problems are treated as people or process problems when the underlying issue is actually information flow.

Data exists, but it is fragmented across spreadsheets, ERP systems, emails, dashboards and operational reports. Teams spend time assembling information instead of using it to make decisions.

My goal is to work at the intersection of these layers: understanding the operational problem, designing the underlying process and system, analysing the resulting data and increasingly using AI to help operations teams move from information to action.

This is the direction behind my work in Dynamics 365 SCM, analytics and the Operations Intelligence Agent I am currently developing.

WHERE I AM GOING

Building the next generation of operational capability.

My long-term objective is to become a specialist at the intersection of supply chain operations, enterprise systems and AI — helping organisations build decision systems that make their operations more visible, measurable and intelligent.
