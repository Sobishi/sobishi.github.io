D365 SCM CASE STUDY

Inventory Accuracy Recovery

A Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management case study demonstrating how inventory accuracy improved from 87% to 98% through structured inventory controls, transaction discipline and Power BI reporting.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Recovering inventory accuracy through process control and system discipline.

This project simulated a multi-site field service spare parts operation and demonstrated how inventory accuracy can be improved through stronger warehouse execution, inventory controls and transaction management.

Dynamics 365 SCM was used to configure inventory processes while Power BI was used to monitor operational performance and inventory accuracy trends.

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Inventory data could no longer be trusted.

Inventory accuracy had declined to approximately 87%, creating stock discrepancies, inventory variances and operational inefficiencies.

The objective was to identify root causes, improve inventory control processes and restore inventory accuracy.
