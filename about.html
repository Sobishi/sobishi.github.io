ABOUT

I understand operations from the inside.

My work sits at the intersection of supply chain, operational systems, analytics and emerging AI.

My professional journey

I am a supply chain and operations professional with eight years of experience across logistics, inventory, warehousing, planning, procurement and operational improvement.

My experience has been built in environments where operational decisions have real consequences: inventory availability, customer service, logistics cost, supplier performance and data accuracy.

I currently manage supply chain operations across multi-site and multi-country networks, including warehouse and field stocking locations, third-party logistics partners and a significant technology inventory portfolio.

What I have learned from operations

One of the biggest lessons from my career is that operational problems are rarely caused by a single department.

Inventory problems can begin with process design. Logistics problems can begin with poor data. Supplier problems can begin with weak commercial controls. And reporting problems can begin with transactions that were never captured correctly.

That experience has changed the way I approach operations. I increasingly look beyond the immediate problem and ask how the underlying process, system, data and decision logic should work together.

Why Operations Intelligence?

I am particularly interested in the space between operational expertise and technology.

A supply chain professional understands the problem. A data professional can analyse the information. A systems professional can configure the workflow. An AI system can potentially help make the resulting intelligence available at the moment a decision is required.

My long-term goal is to bring these capabilities together and build practical Operations Intelligence systems that help organisations make faster, better-informed decisions.
