FEATURED THINKING

The future of supply chain is not just automation.

Automation can make an existing process faster. But if the underlying process is poorly designed, automation can simply make a bad process operate at greater speed.

The more interesting opportunity is designing systems where operational data, business processes and intelligent decision support work together.
