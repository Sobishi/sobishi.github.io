OPERATIONS LAB

Where supply chain meets systems and AI.

The Operations Lab is where I experiment with ideas, workflows and technologies that could make operational decision-making faster, more intelligent and more scalable.

CURRENT BUILD

Operations Intelligence Agent

I am exploring how AI agents can help supply chain and operations teams investigate problems without requiring someone to manually assemble information from multiple systems.

The concept is simple: connect operational data, understand the context behind an issue, investigate possible causes and provide decision support that helps an operations professional determine what to do next.

Supply Chain AI Agents Decision Support Automation Python

THE APPROACH

Start with the operational problem.

I am interested in AI applications where the technology solves a genuine operational problem rather than being added simply because it is available.

My approach is to start with the workflow: understand what happens today, identify where information gets fragmented, determine where decisions slow down and then explore whether automation, analytics or AI can improve the process.

This keeps the technology grounded in measurable operational outcomes.

EXPERIMENT AREAS

What I am exploring.

Inventory Intelligence

Exploring systems that can identify inventory anomalies, investigate stock discrepancies and surface potential root causes.

Supplier Intelligence

Exploring ways to combine supplier performance, purchasing and delivery data to identify emerging supply risks.

Warehouse Intelligence

Experimenting with tools that can analyse warehouse activity and identify process bottlenecks and unusual patterns.

Workflow Automation

Identifying repetitive operational workflows where automation can reduce manual effort and improve control.

ENTERPRISE SYSTEMS

Dynamics 365 SCM as the operational foundation.

A major part of my development is understanding how Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management can provide the transactional foundation for intelligent operations.

I am particularly interested in the relationship between business processes, ERP transactions, operational data and the decision systems that can be built on top of them.

WHAT COMES NEXT

From experiments to usable systems.

The objective of the Lab is not to collect experiments. It is to turn the strongest ideas into working prototypes that demonstrate how technology can solve real operational problems.

As these experiments develop, I will document the problem, architecture, tools, implementation process and lessons learned.
